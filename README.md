# AI Spend Audit

A free web app that analyzes your AI tool subscriptions and identifies overspending with actionable recommendations. Built for founders and engineering managers who want to right-size their AI tool spend without manually researching every vendor's pricing page.

**Live demo:** https://ai-spend-audit-self.vercel.app  
**API:** https://ai-spend-audit-2pzq.onrender.com/api/health

---

## What it does

1. You enter your AI tool subscriptions — tool name, plan, seats, monthly price
2. The audit engine analyzes each subscription against current vendor pricing
3. You get an instant breakdown of where you're overspending and what to switch to
4. Results include total monthly and annual savings estimates
5. Every audit gets a unique shareable URL with Open Graph previews
6. Email capture sends you a permanent link to your report
