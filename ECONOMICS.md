# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

- Average credit purchase: $500-2000 (first transaction)
- Gross margin on credits: ~20-30%
- Gross profit per transaction: ~$250
- Repeat purchase rate (6 months): ~40%
- LTV over 12 months: ~$400

One converted customer is worth approximately $400 in gross profit to Credex.

## CAC at each GTM channel

| Channel | Conversion rate | CAC |
|---------|----------------|-----|
| Hacker News organic | 0.5% visitor to customer | $0 |
| Reddit posts | 0.3% visitor to customer | $0 |
| Indie Hackers | 1% visitor to customer | $0 |
| Direct founder DMs | 5% to audit, 10% to customer | $0 |
| Newsletter sponsorship | 2% click, 3% customer | ~$100/customer |
| Credex existing customers | 15% audit, 20% customer | ~$0 |

## Conversion funnel

- Landing page visit: 100%
- Audit started: 35%
- Audit completed: 25%
- Email captured: 15%
- Credex CTA clicked: 5%
- Consultation booked: 2%
- Credit purchase: 1%

For every 1000 visitors: 250 audits, 37 email captures, 10 consultations booked, 2-3 purchases.

## What makes this profitable

At $400 gross profit per customer and $0 CAC organic, every conversion is pure profit. The tool costs ~$20/month to run on free tiers. Effectively zero marginal cost per audit.

Break-even: 1 customer per month covers infrastructure.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR requires ~2,500 customers at $400 LTV each.

- Month 1-3: Organic seeding, 50 customers
- Month 4-6: Newsletter partnerships, 150 additional customers
- Month 7-12: Credex customer base activation (500 existing, 20% convert = 100). Total: ~400 customers
- Month 13-18: SEO compounds, referral program, widget. 200 new/month. Total: ~1600 customers

What has to be true: audit-to-consultation conversion must be at least 2%. Below 1% the funnel breaks even but does not scale. Above 3% this is a $3M ARR business in 18 months.

The single biggest lever is audit quality. Accurate and surprising recommendations get shared. Generic ones do not.
