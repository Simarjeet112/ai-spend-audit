## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no signup, no catch. We capture your email after showing you results — never before.

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: Every recommendation is based on current vendor pricing with cited sources. The logic is deterministic — if you have 2 seats on ChatGPT Team at $25/seat, we compare that against 2x ChatGPT Plus at $20 total and show you the math. No AI hallucinations in the audit logic.

**Q: What tools do you cover?**
A: ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, Gemini, Notion, Figma, Linear, Vercel, Midjourney, Perplexity, and more. If your tool isn't listed, use "Other" and we'll note it for future coverage.

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit is saved with a unique URL so you can share it. Email is optional and only collected after you see results. We don't sell data.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits sourced from companies that overforecast. If your audit shows significant savings potential, we'll surface Credex as a way to capture even more value beyond plan optimization.
