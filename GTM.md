# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Exact target user

**Primary:** Engineering manager or CTO at a 5-50 person startup, Series A or earlier, who personally approves the SaaS budget. They use 3-8 AI tools, pay monthly, and have never audited whether each tool is right-sized. They feel the spend is "probably fine" but have no benchmark.

**Secondary:** Indie hackers and solo founders running 2-3 AI subscriptions who are cost-conscious by necessity.

The primary user is not the CFO — the CFO doesn't know what Cursor is. It's the technical decision-maker who both uses the tools and controls the budget.

## What they Google before wanting this

- "cursor vs github copilot 2026"
- "chatgpt team plan worth it"
- "how much should a startup spend on AI tools"
- "reduce ai tool costs startup"
- "github copilot enterprise vs business"

These are comparison and pricing queries — people already feeling the spend, not yet knowing there's a tool to help.

## First 100 users in 30 days with $0

**Week 1 — Seeding (days 1-7)**

Post a Show HN: "I built a free tool that audits your AI tool subscriptions." HN converts well for tools with immediate utility. Target front page. One good HN post can drive 500-2000 visitors.

Post on r/SaaS and r/startups: "I was paying $340/month for AI tools. Built an audit tool that found $120/month in savings. Free to use." Personal story format outperforms generic tool announcements.

**Week 2 — Seeding specific communities (days 8-14)**

Post in Indie Hackers with the actual audit results from my own stack. Show the before/after. Link the tool. IH users are exactly the target user — technical, cost-conscious, building products.

DM 20 founders directly on X. Not "check out my tool" — send them a personalized audit of their public stack (most founders tweet about what tools they use). "I noticed you're using ChatGPT Team + Cursor Business — ran your stack through our tool, found $90/month in potential savings. Happy to share the full audit."

**Week 3 — Distribution via existing audiences (days 15-21)**

Reach out to 5 newsletters that cover dev tools (TLDR, Bytes.dev, Cooper Press). Offer a free audit to their audience as a lead magnet. These newsletters convert at 2-5% for free tools.

Post the shareable report URL on Twitter after running a real audit on a well-known tool stack. "Just audited the typical YC startup AI stack — $847/month, $310/month in potential savings. Here's the full breakdown: [link]"

**Week 4 — Compound (days 22-30)**

By now, shareable report URLs are the viral loop. Every user who shares their report is an impression. Add "Audited by AI Spend Audit" to the public report footer with a link. One shared report from a founder with 5k followers = 50-200 new visitors.

## The unfair distribution channel

Credex's existing customer base. Credex already sells AI credits to startups. Every Credex customer is a warm lead — they're already paying for AI tools, already cost-conscious enough to buy credits. An email to the existing customer list ("free tool to audit your AI spend") converts at 10-20% vs 2-3% for cold traffic. No other tool has this distribution shortcut.

- 15-25 email captures
- 5-10 shareable report URLs posted publicly
